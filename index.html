    The Idea Takes Shape

    1. It starts with a room.

      A few pencil lines: walls, a floor, a corner to work with.

    2. Then the pieces arrive.

      A window, a sofa, a lamp, a plant. The room starts to feel lived in.

    3. Notes and colors creep in.

      A width to check, light to keep, a first palette.

    4. So does your client.

      Their wishes arrive as scraps: screenshots, adjectives, a budget, a feeling.

    5. And our first product idea.

      What if you could scan the room and drop the furniture straight in?

    Our First Version Scanned Rooms

    The first HaneiRoom was an AR app. You scanned a room with your phone camera, placed virtual furniture and decor into the space, and could buy the exact piece you placed.

    That version didn't succeed, so we archived it. Real, shoppable furniture came with us. The AR scanning stayed in the box.

    This time we started from the work you already do with every client: the brief.
