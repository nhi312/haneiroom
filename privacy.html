Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

The short version

  • Your camera feed and room scans stay on your phone. We don't upload them.
  • We store your account and your projects so HaneiAI can work, and we send your project brief to an AI provider to find furniture.
  • We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.
  • You can ask us to see, fix, export or delete your data at any time: info.haneiroom@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

HaneiRoom is made by Quan and Nhi, independent developers based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA, and in Taiwan (R.O.C.). "HaneiRoom", "we", "us" and "our" mean the two of us. This policy covers:

  • haneiroom.com, this website;
  • HaneiRoom, our iPhone app that places furniture in your room with augmented reality (AR); and
  • HaneiAI, our web app that turns a client's brief into a furniture list.

Together we call these "the Services".

2. What we collect

When you visit haneiroom.com

  • Demo requests. If you book a demo, we get the email address you type in. The form is handled by Formspree, which stores the request and forwards it to us.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the servers that deliver the site record basic request data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its text uses Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive that request data.
  • The website itself uses no analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own.

When you use HaneiRoom (the iPhone app)

  • Camera and room scans. HaneiRoom uses your camera to find the floor, measure the room and place furniture. This is processed on your device. We don't upload or store your camera feed, photos of your room or the room scan.
  • Account information. If you create an account, we keep your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • Purchases. When you choose "Buy", you continue on the shop's own site or app. We don't collect or store payment card details; the shop handles payment under its own privacy policy.

When you use HaneiAI (the web app)

  • Account information: your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • Project information: what you enter or create for a project, such as the project and client name, style adjectives, color palette, budget, furniture categories, uploaded images and documents, the products you keep or drop, and your comments.
  • Ask AI conversations: the questions you ask about products and the answers.

3. How we use it

  • To run the Services: save your projects, build your brief, find matching products and show your results.
  • To send your project brief, and questions you ask through Ask AI, to our AI provider so it can search for and suggest furniture (see section 4).
  • To reply to demo requests and support emails.
  • To keep the Services secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't use your projects to train our own AI models.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with services that help us run HaneiRoom, and only what they need:

  • AI provider. HaneiAI sends your brief and Ask AI questions to a third-party AI provider, currently Google (Gemini), to search for products and write answers. The provider processes this under its own terms for business customers.
  • Hosting, database and storage providers that keep accounts and projects.
  • Formspree for demo requests, and GitHub and Google Fonts for delivering this website.
  • Shops and vendors: when you open a vendor's page or buy a piece, you leave HaneiRoom and that shop's privacy policy applies.
  • Legal reasons: if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or ourselves.

If HaneiRoom is ever sold or merged, your information may move to the new owner, who must keep honoring this policy.

5. Your clients' information

Designers may enter details about their clients in HaneiAI, such as a client's name, style preferences, budget or photos. Please only add what you need and what your client is happy for you to share. We use it only to run your project, and we delete it when you delete the project or ask us to.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and project information: for as long as your account is open. When you delete a project or your account, we delete the related data from our active systems within 30 days, and from backups when they cycle out.
  • Demo request emails: until the demo is done and we have no further need to follow up, or sooner if you ask.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it for a particular purpose. Email info.haneiroom@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act give you these rights; we offer them to everyone. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

You can turn off HaneiRoom's camera access at any time in your iPhone's Settings, though the AR features won't work without it.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and reputable providers to protect your information. No service is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that information will never be accessed without permission. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

9. Children

The Services are made for interior designers and their clients and are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We don't knowingly collect children's information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

10. International transfers

We work from the United States and Taiwan, and our providers may process information in other countries. Wherever it is processed, we protect it as this policy describes.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also let account holders know by email or in the app before it takes effect.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: info.haneiroom@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Service.
