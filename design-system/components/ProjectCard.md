# ProjectCard
A saved project in the homepage and My Projects lists: title, client, created date, status, palette swatches and an open button.

- Consumer provides: title, client name, created date, status (Draft or Done), up to five swatch colors, and the open handler.
- White, `radius-lg`, `shadow-md`, 16 by 20px padding. Title in bold body, metadata in `text-muted`.
- Stack cards 12px apart inside a glass panel; page with a Next Page pill after four.
