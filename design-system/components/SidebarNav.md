# SidebarNav
The desktop app's left sidebar: profile chip and the Home, My Projects and My Inbox rows.

- Consumer provides: the user's avatar, name and role, the nav items (icon, label, href) and which one is active.
- 240px wide. The active row gets an orange-500 glow fading to transparent from the left. Icons come from the Icons group at 18px.
- In the app a survey card and Setting sit below the nav. On the landing page, use this only inside product shots.
