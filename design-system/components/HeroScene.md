# HeroScene
The signature Hanei composition: a real product photo standing in a hand-drawn line room, with glass cards and stickers floating around it. Use it for the hero, How It Works steps and feature sections.

- Consumer provides: one cut-out product photo (transparent PNG), a title, one call to action, up to three cards (project, brief, pre-check) and up to two stickers.
- Build it in five layers, back to front: Doodles group line art in `doodle-ink`, the product photo, the title and CTA, glass cards, stickers.
- The photo is the only real image in the scene and always sits on the room-floor doodle, centered, with a soft drop shadow.
- Doodles stay behind the photo and cards. Use 6 to 12 per scene: a room-floor under the photo, squiggles at the sides, a few stars or bursts above, objects (plant, table, lamp, window) at the edges.
- Cards are glass (`glass-white`, `glass-edge`, `shadow-glass`, 16px blur), tilted between −4° and 4°. The pre-check card uses red-100 at 82% with red-700 text.
- Stickers sit on a card corner, never on the photo. Underline one title word with the swoosh doodle.
- On mobile, drop to the photo, the floor, two squiggles and one card.
