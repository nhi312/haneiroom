# Chip
Toggle chip for picking furniture types, plus a counter chip, from the desktop intake.

- Consumer provides: the label, the picked state (`aria-pressed`) and the change handler; for the counter, the picked and maximum counts.
- Unpicked: grey-100 with ink-900 text. Picked: navy-700 with white text. Counter: white with `shadow-sm`.
- Pill radius, 28px tall, `caption` text. Max 10 picks in the app.
