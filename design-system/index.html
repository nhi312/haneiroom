Brand colors

navy-700#253a82

Brand navy, exact from the app's palette swatch (#253A82). Titles, navy pill buttons in their active state, links, every line icon. 10.5:1 on white, 8.2:1 on grey-100, 6.1:1 on the lavender app gradient.

navy-300#93a4de

Periwinkle, exact from the app's palette swatch (#93A4DE). Back-button circle, swatches, gradient tints. Decorative only: never text on it.

orange-500#ff912c

Brand orange, exact from the app's palette swatch (#FF912C). CTA fill and the New Project hover state. Label on it is brown-900 (6.1:1). The app's own white-on-orange label is 2.3:1 and fails; don't carry it to the web.

orange-300#fcc490

Peach-orange, exact from the app's palette swatch (#FCC490). CTA gradient start, warm gradient stop. Label on it is brown-900 (8.8:1).

rust-600#a85a22

Second line of two-line titles, as on the splash screen ("Dream Space"). 4.1:1 on peach-100 and lavender-100, so use it at 24px and up only; 5.1:1 on white.

brown-900#3b2a14

Warm ink: labels on orange buttons, settings headings, sticker outlines. 11.2:1 on lavender-100.

lavender-100#e6e7f8

List-row and step-card fills, the palm-pattern ground.

lavender-200#d5d8f2

Background glows and the cool end of page gradients.

peach-100#fbe3cc

Warm end of page gradients; ground for warm sections.

grey-100#e4e4e4

Default page ground, as on the app screens.

grey-300#c9c9c9

Input fields, image placeholder tiles, hairlines.

grey-600#5c5c5c

Muted text: captions, helper text, metadata. 5.3:1 on grey-100.

ink-900#1e1e1e

Body text on grey-100, white and lavender-100.

white#ffffff

Cards, secondary buttons, sticker die-cut edge, text on navy-700.

green-500#5db85b

Success chip fill and the keep check. Text on it is ink-900 (6.7:1).

red-500#e04545

Remove icon and the conflict alert's icon. Not for text.

red-700#8f2a22

Text inside the conflict alert, on red-100.

red-100#f8c9c9

Conflict alert background.

palm-600#3f7a4e

Palm leaves in stickers and the palm pattern. 5.1:1 on white if ever used for a mark.

palm-300#7fb07a

Light leaves in stickers and the palm pattern.

logo-navy#273d93

Ink of "Hanei" and the hard hat in the logo. Only the logo uses it; UI uses navy-700.

logo-orange#d67a31

Ink of "Ai" in the logo. Only the logo uses it. 3.1:1 on white, so never small text.

rust-700#933519

Hover and open state of the desktop View-all and Menu buttons, with white text or icon (7.6:1). Sampled from a gradient fill, so confirm in Dev Mode.

Semantic colors

text-primary{ink-900}

Body copy on bg-page, surface-card and surface-tint.

text-heading{navy-700}

Headlines and section titles on any light ground.

text-accent{rust-600}

Second line of two-line titles, 24px and up.

text-highlight{orange-500}

One highlighted word in a title (40px and up), e.g. Hanei.

text-muted{grey-600}

Captions and helper text on bg-page and surface-card.

bg-page{grey-100}

Default page background.

surface-card{white}

Cards and glass panels (use at 70 to 90% opacity with a 20px background blur for glass).

surface-tint{lavender-100}

List rows, step cards.

action-primary{orange-500}

Main CTA fill (gradient from orange-300). Label: on-action-primary.

on-action-primary{brown-900}

Label on action-primary.

action-secondary{navy-700}

Navy pill buttons (Next, Ask AI). Label: white.

focus-ring{navy-700}

2px solid focus ring with a 2px offset; 7.8:1 on grey-100, 9.9:1 on white, 3.9:1 on orange-500.
