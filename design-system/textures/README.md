Backgrounds and overlays. gradient-warm (hero, final CTA), gradient-cool (feature sections) and gradient-navy-glow (footer, one dark band) are 1440×900 full-bleed grounds: use `background-size: cover`. grain and paper are transparent overlays: lay them over a gradient at 40 to 60% with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`. palm-pattern-tile repeats at 240px on lavender-100. palm-shadow is a transparent 800px overlay for a section corner. One pattern per section.
